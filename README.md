# 🧠 20 Questions to Uniquely Identify You – User Guide

Welcome to the **"20 Questions" AI Game!**  
This interactive web app asks a series of smart questions to figure out how **unique** you are — all in 20 questions.

🔗 **Start here:**  
[Click me!](https://coolguywithapen.github.io/v2-ai-20-questions/)

---

## 💡 What This Is

This app is a fun social experiment inspired by the classic “20 Questions” game and the science of **information theory**.

It works by asking you questions that are designed to quickly narrow down your answers from everyone else’s.  
You’ll get a **“uniqueness score”** after all the response — try to be the hardest person to identify!

---

## ✅ How to Play

1. Click the link to start the quiz.
2. Answer each question honestly.
3. After all the questions, your **uniqueness score** will update.

---

## 🔍 What the Uniqueness Score Means

- **Low score** (like `10`) → many people answer like you
- **High score** (like `90`) → you're pretty unique!

The score shows how rare your combination of answers is.

---

## ✨ Why It’s Fun

- Compete with friends to see who’s more “basic” or “one of a kind”
- Discover how predictable (or unpredictable) your choices are

---

## 🔒 Privacy First

- No account or login required
- No name, email, or personal data collected
- All answers are anonymous and used only to calculate uniqueness

---

## 💬 Tips & Tricks

- Don’t try to “game” the system — answer naturally
- Answer with semi-long answers
